Our Story

If someone had told us back in 2015, when we were 17 and sitting in the same college classroom, that we'd be getting married someday, we probably wouldn't have believed them!

What started as a friendship somehow turned into love, and here we are, more than a decade later!

Since then, we've moved countries, chased our dreams, survived long-distance, and figured out adulthood together (still figuring it out, actually!). We've been through the good days, the not-so-good days, and everything in between. Through it all, we've grown together, made countless memories, and remained best friends.

Along the way, our worlds have come together too, bringing two cultures, different traditions, and both our families into one big part of our story.

And now, those same two college kids are getting married! It's still a little surreal, but we couldn't be more excited for what's ahead. We can't wait to celebrate with all our favourite people.

Here's to everything we've been and everything we're yet to become. ❤️
